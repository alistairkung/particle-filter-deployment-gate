# Use a compact queue state and the three experimentally supported regimes

**Approximate Markov state.** Use $X_t=(Z_t,W_t)$ at the five-second boundaries. The joint regime $Z_t$ specifies the background query intensity and publisher acknowledgement condition. $W_t$ is unfinished DB work. This preserves the three physical quantities in [ADR 0004](0004-particle-workload-and-publisher-memory.md) and keeps their inference interpretable within the MSc project. The ordered DB queue, outstanding event timestamps, publisher phase and residual acknowledgement time are omitted. Equal states can therefore yield different next marks, pending ages and future DB admissions. A likelihood that averages poorly over that memory could give overconfident regime estimates, biased estimates of unfinished work, and miscalibrated hold or promote forecasts. Predictive sufficiency remains an empirical question.

**Restricted operating regimes.** The primary state space contains A (3.5 background queries/s, 2 ms acknowledgement), B (5.5/s, 2 ms) and C (3.5/s, 50 ms). The calibration and ordinary evaluation designs cover these combinations. Restricting the model to them bounds the study and makes its likelihoods interpretable on the observed support. Simultaneous elevated demand and slow publishing are physically possible in production and outside primary scope. Faced with both, the model must assign its posterior among A, B and C. It could misattribute the outbox signal to one disturbance, distort the inferred queue and either action risk, or produce narrow uncertainty despite poor fit.

All methods receive the same regime-space and workload assumptions. The evaluator keeps actual regime labels and intervention timing private. Five-second pre-decision transition probabilities require justification independent of experimental condition frequencies and the fixed intervention schedule. [ADR 0006](0006-pre-decision-regime-half-life.md) records the departure probability. The primary continuations preserve each sampled regime and evolve stochastic workloads and queues under [ADR 0003](0003-regime-persistence-in-action-forecasts.md).

Challenge omitted memory on development roots before proposing the smallest evidence-supported augmentation. Report results within each condition and compare forecasts with and without history-specific unfinished-work estimates beyond regime recognition. A combined-disturbance robustness scenario is optional and reported separately. The [working specification and final-report limitations plan](../../particle_filter/SCIENTIFIC_SPEC.md) set out how to assess both assumptions.
