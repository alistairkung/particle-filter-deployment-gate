# Forecast hold and promote with one matched future per joint posterior draw

For each forecast pair, draw the regime and unfinished work together from the weighted decision-time posterior. Reconstruct compatible publisher progress from the approved approximation and delivered outbox history once. Both branches inherit this draw, including work already admitted to the DB. The reconstruction must account for each outstanding visit exactly once. It approximates the unobserved queue order and publisher progress, so errors at the cut can affect both action risks and their difference.

Draw fresh future primitives independently across forecast pairs and independently of evaluator randomness. Within a pair, share external arrivals, background service demands, request routing uniforms and business-work draws. The 25% hold and 60% promote branches keep the sampled regime for 120 seconds, apply their respective routing thresholds to new writes and evolve their own FIFO and publisher. Forecasters receive only permitted history and declared controls and workload assumptions. The evaluator's cut state and future streams stay private.

Score both branches against the frozen write endpoint, retain all four paired outcomes and report the two marginal breach frequencies and their signed difference. Choose the number of forecast pairs on development roots from convergence across independent forecast seeds. Report that stability and validate each action's calibration across low- and high-backlog histories. The independent evaluation unit is a root.
