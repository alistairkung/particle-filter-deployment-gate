# Independent scientific specification review

**Disposition, 25 November 2026.** Two separate read-only agent reviewers assessed the design on simulator baseline `aa48b7a` and rechecked the revisions. Both judged their reviewed portions ready for owner approval. This review covers scientific design and implementation readiness. Physical calibration, runtime, numerical convergence and independent verification of a future implementation require experimental evidence.

| Material review finding | Resolution verified by the reviewers |
|---|---|
| A transition before the first interval would change the approved starting regime law. Late evidence could also leak into its own preceding transition. | Use the uniform draw during interval one. Record each transition's original public context and score delayed evidence on retained ancestry at delivery. |
| Rescaling callback times could shorten a queued write below its insert cost or stretch a fixed-duration publisher visit. Some regime, count and work combinations lack development examples. | Use typed residual visits, exact total-work conservation, fixed service constraints and compatible callbacks. Monitor use and bias of the sparse-cell fallback. Reconstruct both ablation and full draws under the same physical rules. |
| Empty timing categories and impossible count/age combinations could create spurious or zero weights. Easy recognition of slow publishing could dominate state metrics. | Normalise on feasible support, smooth sparse legal timing bands, and check the regime-specific publisher capacity and FIFO age constraints. Examine A versus B and within-regime work estimation separately. |
| Finite forecast simulation adds loss to the PF comparison, while the locked temporal predictor is deterministic. A bootstrap interval can have zero width on degenerate paired losses. | Evaluate the delivered finite-budget pipeline, measure its seed variance on development, and require positive estimated pooled sampling variance for directional inference. Report achieved precision. |
| Different history summaries limit attribution, and changing work in the ablation can invalidate a copied publisher state. | Attribute the primary result to the complete pipeline. Retain the same regime probabilities and publisher reconstruction rule in the ablation, with obligations compatible with each work draw. Define positive ablation gain as ablated minus full signed-increment MSE. |

The reviewers examined the [methods and implementation plan](METHODS_PLAN.md), [scientific specification](SCIENTIFIC_SPEC.md) and [evaluation plan](EVALUATION_PLAN.md). Outstanding empirical questions are whether the compact closure and likelihood validate on held-out development roots, which numerical budgets converge in time, and whether the full run fits the available resources. After this review, the owner approved revised Decision 12 with 300 fresh protected roots, balanced across A/B/C. The independent post-implementation verification remains a separate milestone.
