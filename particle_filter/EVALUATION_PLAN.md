## Scoring and interpretation

Commit a fresh root namespace, equal A/B/C counts, a complete inventory and disjoint evaluator and forecast RNG streams. Keep root families intact. For each root let D be its matched promote breach indicator minus its hold breach indicator. Compute the paired loss difference g = (temporal increment − D)² − (PF increment − D)². Average g within condition and weight the three condition means equally. Positive gain favours the full PF pipeline.

Resample whole roots 10,000 times within each condition with fixed fits and forecasts. Preserve pairings across methods, actions and sensitivities.
